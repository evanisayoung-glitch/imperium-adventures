## Primary logo

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| [`logo/imperium-adventures-logo.svg`](./logo/imperium-adventures-logo.svg) | Primary vector logo (preferred) |

| [`logo/imperium-adventures-logo-web.png`](./logo/imperium-adventures-logo-web.png) | Optimized web raster |

### Emblem

Circular crest with mountain peak, compass rose, crown, and laurel — forest green and gold on off-white.

### Wordmark

- **IMPERIUM** — primary display line  
- **ADVENTURES** — secondary line with gold rules  
- **LLC** — legal designation in gold  

## Usage notes

- Prefer the full logo on light backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the emblem at least the height of the crown.
- Do not recolor the crest outside the approved palette without a dedicated brand revision.
